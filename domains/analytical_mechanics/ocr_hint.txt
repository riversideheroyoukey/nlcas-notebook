## OCR Domain Hint: Analytical Mechanics

When recognizing handwritten mathematical expressions in this domain:

Symbol recognition priorities:
- Single-dot overhead (q̇) → LaTeX: \dot{q}  — generalized velocity
- Double-dot overhead (q̈) → LaTeX: \ddot{q}  — generalized acceleration
- δ (lowercase delta, cursive) → variational operator \delta, NOT partial ∂
- ∂ → partial derivative \partial
- L (script or upright) → Lagrangian L
- H (script or upright) → Hamiltonian H
- Subscript i on coordinates → q_i, p_i

Common expression patterns:
- Fraction with ∂L in numerator → \frac{\partial L}{\partial q}
- d/dt(...) → \frac{d}{dt}\left(...\right)
- Curly brace expressions → Poisson bracket \{f, g\}
