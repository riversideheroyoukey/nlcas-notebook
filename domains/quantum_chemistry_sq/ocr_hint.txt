## OCR Domain Hint: Quantum Chemistry (Second Quantization)

Symbol recognition priorities:
- † (dagger) after letter → creation operator a^{\dagger}_p
- p, q, r, s as subscripts in orbital context → orbital indices p, q, r, s
- h with two subscripts (pq) → one-electron integral h_{pq}
- g with four subscripts (pqrs) → two-electron integral g_{pqrs}
- Σ with orbital subscript → sum over orbitals \sum_{pq}
- |HF⟩ or |Φ_0⟩ → Hartree-Fock reference \ket{\mathrm{HF}}
- ε with subscript p → orbital energy \varepsilon_p
- T (hat or italic, followed by subscript 1 or 2) → cluster operator \hat{T}_1, \hat{T}_2
- Curly brackets {A, B} → anticommutator
- Square brackets [A, B] → commutator
- ⟨...|...|...⟩ → matrix element \langle \cdot | \cdot | \cdot \rangle
