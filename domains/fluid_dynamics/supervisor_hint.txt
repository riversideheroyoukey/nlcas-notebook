## Domain: Fluid Dynamics

Common symbols and their roles:
- u, v, w (or bold **u**): velocity field components — functions of (x, y, z, t)
- ρ: fluid density
- p: pressure field — function of (x, y, z, t)
- T: temperature or stress tensor
- ν: kinematic viscosity (constant)
- μ: dynamic viscosity (constant)
- Re: Reynolds number (dimensionless parameter)
- ∇, ∇²: gradient and Laplacian — operate on spatial coordinates

Typical dependency patterns to infer:
- Velocity components u, v, w depend on (x, y, z, t)
- Scalar fields (ρ, p, T) depend on (x, y, z, t) unless stated as constant
- Vorticity ω = ∇ × u — depends on same variables as u

When the user writes "x で偏微分", interpret as ∂/∂x.
