## OCR Domain Hint: Fluid Dynamics

When recognizing handwritten mathematical expressions in this domain:

Symbol recognition priorities:
- Bold u, v, w → vector fields \mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}, \mathbf{w}
- ∇ (nabla) → \nabla  — gradient/divergence/curl operator
- ∇² or Δ → \nabla^2  — Laplacian
- ρ (rho) → density \rho, NOT p
- ν (nu) → kinematic viscosity \nu, NOT v
- ∂/∂x, ∂/∂y, ∂/∂z → partial derivatives w.r.t. spatial coordinates

Common expression patterns:
- ∂u/∂t + (u·∇)u → Navier-Stokes convective term
- Circled dot (⊗) or crossed arrows → tensor product
- Contour integral symbol (∮) → line/surface integral
