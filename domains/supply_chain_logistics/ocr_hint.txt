## OCR Domain Hint: Supply Chain & Logistics

Symbol recognition priorities:
- Q → order quantity;  D → demand;  F(·) → demand CDF
- cu, co (subscripted c) → underage / overage cost
- σ (sigma) → demand std;  L → lead time;  z → safety factor
- SS → safety stock;  S → order-up-to level;  μ → mean demand

Common expression patterns:
- cu/(cu+co) → critical ratio (newsvendor)
- F(Q) = cu/(cu+co) → newsvendor optimality
- SS = zσ√L,  S = μL + zσ√L → safety stock / base-stock
