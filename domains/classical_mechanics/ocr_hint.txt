## OCR Domain Hint: Classical (Newtonian) Mechanics

When recognizing handwritten mathematical expressions in this domain:

Symbol recognition priorities:
- Single-dot overhead (ẋ) → LaTeX: \dot{x}  — velocity dx/dt
- Double-dot overhead (ẍ) → LaTeX: \ddot{x}  — acceleration d²x/dt²
- F → force;  m → mass;  g → gravitational acceleration
- p → momentum;  v → velocity;  a → acceleration
- Bold or arrow over symbol → vector quantity (\vec{F}, \vec{r})
- Subscripts (x, y, z) on vectors → components (F_x, v_y)

Common expression patterns:
- F = m a  → Newton's second law
- d/dt(...) → \frac{d}{dt}\left(...\right)
- Integral over t → time integration of equations of motion
- ½ m v² → kinetic energy;  m g h → gravitational potential energy
