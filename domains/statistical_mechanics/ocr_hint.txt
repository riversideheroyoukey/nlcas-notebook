## OCR Domain Hint: Statistical Mechanics & Thermodynamics

Symbol recognition priorities:
- Z (standalone, not a coordinate) → partition function Z
- β → inverse temperature \beta = 1/(k_B T), NOT a coefficient
- k_B or k with subscript B → Boltzmann constant k_B
- μ in thermodynamics context → chemical potential \mu, NOT dynamic viscosity
- ∂F/∂T, ∂G/∂P → thermodynamic partial derivatives
- Σ_n exp(-βE_n) → partition function sum
- 〈...〉 → thermal average \langle ... \rangle

Common expression patterns:
- F = -k_BT ln Z → Helmholtz free energy
- S = -∂F/∂T → entropy from free energy
- ⟨E⟩ = -∂ ln Z/∂β → average energy
