## Domain: Statistical Mechanics & Thermodynamics

Common symbols and their roles:
- Z: partition function (canonical: Z = Σ exp(-βEₙ))
- β = 1/(k_BT): inverse temperature
- k_B: Boltzmann constant (scalar)
- F = -k_BT ln Z: Helmholtz free energy
- G: Gibbs free energy
- S: entropy
- μ: chemical potential
- T: temperature (scalar, independent variable)
- N: number of particles
- V: volume
- P: pressure

Typical dependency patterns:
- Thermodynamic potentials F, G, S depend on (T, V, N) or (T, P, N)
- Partition function Z depends on (β, {parameters})
- Correlation functions depend on distance r or time t

When the user writes "T で微分", interpret as ∂/∂T (temperature derivative).
