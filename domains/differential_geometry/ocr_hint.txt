## OCR Domain Hint: Differential Geometry

Symbol recognition priorities:
- Γ with one upper and two lower indices → Christoffel symbol \Gamma^k_{ij}
- ∇ with subscript μ or i → covariant derivative \nabla_\mu
- ω with superscript → differential form \omega^i
- ∧ between forms → wedge product \wedge
- d before a symbol → exterior derivative d
- T with mixed indices (upper and lower) → tensor T^r_s
- g_{ij} (metric components) → \mathbf{g} with index notation

Common expression patterns:
- ∇_μ V^ν = ∂_μ V^ν + Γ^ν_{μλ} V^λ → covariant derivative of vector
- dω = 0 → closed form (Poincaré lemma)
