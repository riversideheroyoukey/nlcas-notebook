Consider an M/M/1 queue with arrival rate $\lambda$ and service rate $\mu$. Define the utilization $\rho=\lambda/\mu$ (the fraction of time the server is busy).

$\rho = \lambda/\mu$

\begin{equation}
  \rho = \lambda/\mu
  \label{eq:E1}
\end{equation}

Define the mean number in system $L=\rho/(1-\rho)$.

$L = \rho/(1-\rho)$

\begin{equation}
  L = \rho/(1-\rho)
  \label{eq:E2}
\end{equation}

Replace $\rho$ with $\lambda/\mu$ so that $L$ is expressed purely in terms of $\lambda,\mu$.

In E2, substitute $\lambda/\mu$ for $\rho$

\begin{equation}
  L = -\frac{\lambda}{\lambda-\mu}
  \label{eq:E3}
\end{equation}

To apply Little's law $L=\lambda W$, divide both sides by the arrival rate $\lambda$ to obtain the mean time in system $W=L/\lambda$.

Divide both sides of E3 by $\lambda$

\begin{equation}
  \frac{L}{\lambda} = -\frac{1}{\lambda-\mu}
  \label{eq:E4}
\end{equation}
