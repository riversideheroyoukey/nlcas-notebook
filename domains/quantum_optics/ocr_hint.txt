## OCR Domain Hint: Quantum Optics

Symbol recognition priorities:
- † (dagger) after letter → creation operator a^{\dagger}
- Overbar on α → coherent state amplitude \bar{\alpha}
- σ with +/- superscript → \sigma^+, \sigma^-
- σz → \sigma_z (Pauli z-matrix / qubit energy operator)
- ω with subscript c → cavity frequency \omega_c
- κ (kappa) → cavity decay \kappa
- γ (gamma) → spontaneous emission \gamma
- g (italic) in coupling context → Jaynes-Cummings g
- |n⟩ → Fock state \ket{n}
- Square brackets [A, B] → commutator
- ⟨...⟩ with 0 subscript → vacuum expectation value \langle \cdot \rangle_0
