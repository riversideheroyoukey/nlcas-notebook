## Domain: Linear Algebra

Common symbols and their roles:
- A, B, M: matrices (bold uppercase or plain uppercase)
- **v**, **u**, **x**: column vectors (bold lowercase)
- λ: eigenvalue (scalar)
- **v**: eigenvector (associated with eigenvalue λ)
- A^T: transpose of A
- A^{-1}: inverse of A
- A^†, A^*: conjugate transpose (Hermitian adjoint)
- det(A), |A|: determinant
- tr(A): trace
- rank(A): rank
- I, I_n: identity matrix
- 0: zero matrix or zero vector
- ker(A), im(A): kernel and image (null space, column space)

Operator conventions:
- Av = λv: eigenvalue equation
- A = PDP^{-1}: diagonalization
- A = UΣV^T: singular value decomposition

When the user writes "固有値を求めて", interpret as solving det(A - λI) = 0.
