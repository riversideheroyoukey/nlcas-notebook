Register the symmetric matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$.

$A = [2, 1; 1, 2]$

\begin{equation}
  A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}
  \label{eq:E1}
\end{equation}

Find the eigenvalues of $A$.

Find the eigenvalues of E1

\begin{equation}
  \left\{ 3 , 1 \right\}
  \label{eq:E2}
\end{equation}

Find the eigenvectors of $A$.

Find the eigenvectors of E1

\begin{equation}
  \left[ \left[ \left[ 3 , 1 \right]  , \left[ 1 , 1 \right]  \right]  , \left[ \left[ \left[ 1 , 1 \right]  \right]  , \left[ \left[ 1 , -1 \right]  \right]  \right]  \right]
  \label{eq:E3}
\end{equation}

Find the orthogonal transformation matrix $P$ whose columns are the normalized eigenvectors (the matrix that diagonalizes $A$).

Diagonalize E1

\begin{equation}
  \ifx\endpmatrix\undefined\pmatrix{\else\begin{pmatrix}\fi \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}&\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\cr \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}&-\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)\cr \ifx\endpmatrix\undefined}\else\end{pmatrix}\fi
  \label{eq:E4}
\end{equation}

Find the diagonal matrix $D$ with the eigenvalues on its diagonal.

Find the diagonal matrix of E1

\begin{equation}
  \ifx\endpmatrix\undefined\pmatrix{\else\begin{pmatrix}\fi 3&0\cr 0&1\cr \ifx\endpmatrix\undefined}\else\end{pmatrix}\fi
  \label{eq:E5}
\end{equation}

To compute the similarity transform $P^{-1}AP$, first find the inverse $P^{-1}$ of $P$ (since $P$ is orthogonal, $P^{-1}$ should equal $P^\top$).

Find the inverse of E4

\begin{equation}
  \ifx\endpmatrix\undefined\pmatrix{\else\begin{pmatrix}\fi \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}&\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\cr \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}&-\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)\cr \ifx\endpmatrix\undefined}\else\end{pmatrix}\fi
  \label{eq:E6}
\end{equation}

Find the matrix product $P^{-1}A$.

Find the matrix product of E6 and E1

\begin{equation}
  \ifx\endpmatrix\undefined\pmatrix{\else\begin{pmatrix}\fi \frac{3}{\sqrt{2}}&\frac{3}{\sqrt{2}}\cr \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}&-\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)\cr \ifx\endpmatrix\undefined}\else\end{pmatrix}\fi
  \label{eq:E7}
\end{equation}

Multiply by $P$ to complete $P^{-1}AP$. This should match the diagonal matrix $D$ from earlier (the eigenvalues on the diagonal).
