## OCR Domain Hint: Mathematical Economics

Symbol recognition priorities:
- U(x₁, x₂) or U(x) → utility function U
- ℒ or L (script/calligraphic) → Lagrangian \mathcal{L}
- λ next to constraint → Lagrange multiplier \lambda
- p·x or Σ pᵢxᵢ → budget constraint (dot product)
- MRS, MRT → marginal rate of substitution/transformation
- ∂U/∂xᵢ → marginal utility
- ≥, ≤ in constraint context → inequality constraint

Common expression patterns:
- max U(x) s.t. p·x = m → consumer optimization
- ∂ℒ/∂xᵢ = 0 → first-order optimality conditions
- ∂U/∂x₁ / ∂U/∂x₂ = p₁/p₂ → MRS = price ratio at optimum
