## Domain: Mathematical Economics

Common symbols and their roles:
- U(x), U(x₁, x₂, ...): utility function — depends on consumption bundle x
- x = (x₁, x₂, ..., xₙ): consumption vector
- p = (p₁, p₂, ..., pₙ): price vector (parameters)
- m, I: income / budget (parameter)
- ℒ: Lagrangian for constrained optimization
- λ: Lagrange multiplier
- MRS = -dx₂/dx₁: marginal rate of substitution
- f(K, L): production function — depends on capital K and labor L
- C(w, q): cost function — depends on input prices w and output q
- π: profit

KKT conditions:
- ∂ℒ/∂xᵢ = 0 for each good i
- Budget constraint: p·x = m (binding at optimum)

Typical dependency patterns:
- U depends on the consumption bundle (x₁, ..., xₙ)
- f (production function) depends on (K, L) or factor inputs

When the user writes "効用最大化", interpret as max U(x) subject to p·x = m.
