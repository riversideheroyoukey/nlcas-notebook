## OCR Domain Hint: Financial Mathematics

Symbol recognition priorities:
- dW, dWₜ, dB → stochastic differential \, dW_t (Wiener increment)
- dS = ... dt + ... dW → stochastic differential equation
- σ (lowercase) in finance context → volatility \sigma, NOT stress tensor
- μ (lowercase) in SDE context → drift \mu, NOT chemical potential
- S(t) or Sₜ → asset price process S_t
- ∂V/∂S, ∂²V/∂S² → Greeks (Delta, Gamma)
- e^{-rT} → discount factor
- 𝔼[...] or E^Q[...] → risk-neutral expectation

Common expression patterns:
- dS = μS dt + σS dW → geometric Brownian motion
- ∂V/∂t + ½σ²S²∂²V/∂S² + rS∂V/∂S - rV = 0 → Black-Scholes PDE
- df = ∂f/∂t dt + ∂f/∂S dS + ½∂²f/∂S² (dS)² → Itô's formula applied
