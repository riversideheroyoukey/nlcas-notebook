## Domain: Financial Mathematics

Common symbols and their roles:
- S(t), Sₜ: asset price process — function of t (stochastic)
- W(t), Wₜ, Bₜ: standard Brownian motion (Wiener process)
- μ: drift coefficient (constant or deterministic)
- σ: volatility (constant or deterministic)
- r: risk-free interest rate (constant)
- V(S, t): option price — depends on (S, t)
- K: strike price (constant)
- T: maturity date (constant)
- dS = μS dt + σS dW: geometric Brownian motion

Itô's formula:
- For f(S,t): df = (∂f/∂t + μS ∂f/∂S + ½σ²S² ∂²f/∂S²) dt + σS ∂f/∂S dW

Black-Scholes PDE:
- ∂V/∂t + ½σ²S² ∂²V/∂S² + rS ∂V/∂S - rV = 0

Typical dependency patterns:
- Option price V depends on (S, t)
- Stochastic processes S, W depend on t

When the user writes "伊藤の補題を適用して", identify the function f(S,t) and apply Itô's formula.
