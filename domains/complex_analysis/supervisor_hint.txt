## Domain: Complex Analysis

Common symbols and their roles:
- z = x + iy: complex variable
- w = f(z): complex-valued function
- Re(z), Im(z): real and imaginary parts
- |z|: modulus (absolute value)
- arg(z): argument (angle)
- z̄: complex conjugate
- ∮_C: contour integral along curve C
- Res[f, z₀]: residue of f at z₀
- i: imaginary unit (i² = -1)
- ℂ: complex number field
- D, U: domain (open connected subset of ℂ)

Key theorems to recognize:
- Cauchy integral formula: f(z₀) = (1/2πi) ∮ f(z)/(z-z₀) dz
- Residue theorem: ∮ f(z) dz = 2πi Σ Res[f, zₖ]

Typical dependency patterns:
- Analytic functions f depend on z (NOT on x and y separately)
- Laurent series expansions are in powers of (z - z₀)

When the user writes "留数を求めて", interpret as computing Res[f, z₀] = lim_{z→z₀} (z-z₀)f(z) for simple poles.
