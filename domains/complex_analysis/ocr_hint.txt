## OCR Domain Hint: Complex Analysis

Symbol recognition priorities:
- ∮ (closed contour integral) → \oint_C
- z, w (italic, single letter in complex context) → complex variables z, w
- z̄ (z with overbar) → complex conjugate \bar{z}
- Re(...), Im(...) → \text{Re}(...), \text{Im}(...)
- |z| (absolute value of complex) → modulus |z|
- Res[f, z₀] → residue \text{Res}[f, z_0]
- i (imaginary unit, small) → i (NOT index in this context)
- ℂ → complex field \mathbb{C}

Common expression patterns:
- ∮_C f(z) dz = 2πi Σ Res → residue theorem
- f(z₀) = (1/2πi) ∮ f(z)/(z-z₀) dz → Cauchy integral formula
