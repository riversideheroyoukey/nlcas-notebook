## OCR Domain Hint: Game Theory

Symbol recognition priorities:
- uᵢ or πᵢ → payoff function of player i u_i or \pi_i
- s_{-i} (subscript -i) → strategy profile of all players except i s_{-i}
- BR_i(...) → best response correspondence \text{BR}_i
- Δ(S) → simplex of mixed strategies \Delta(S)
- σᵢ (lowercase sigma with subscript) → mixed strategy \sigma_i
- 2×2 matrix with payoff pairs → normal form game (use tabular or matrix notation)
- ≥ in optimization context → preference or constraint

Common expression patterns:
- (s₁*, s₂*): uᵢ(sᵢ*, s_{-i}*) ≥ uᵢ(sᵢ, s_{-i}*) → Nash equilibrium condition
- max_{sᵢ} uᵢ(sᵢ, s_{-i}) → best response problem
