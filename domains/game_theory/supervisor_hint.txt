## Domain: Game Theory

Common symbols and their roles:
- N = {1, 2, ..., n}: set of players
- Sᵢ: strategy set of player i
- S = S₁ × S₂ × ... × Sₙ: strategy profile space
- sᵢ ∈ Sᵢ: pure strategy of player i
- σᵢ: mixed strategy (probability distribution over Sᵢ)
- uᵢ(s), πᵢ(s): payoff function of player i
- s* = (s₁*, ..., sₙ*): Nash equilibrium strategy profile
- BR_i(s_{-i}): best response correspondence of player i
- v(S): characteristic function (coalitional games)
- Δ(Sᵢ): simplex of mixed strategies over Sᵢ

Nash equilibrium condition:
- uᵢ(sᵢ*, s_{-i}*) ≥ uᵢ(sᵢ, s_{-i}*) for all sᵢ ∈ Sᵢ

Typical dependency patterns:
- Payoffs uᵢ depend on the full strategy profile s = (s₁,...,sₙ)
- Mixed strategy payoffs depend on σ = (σ₁,...,σₙ)

When the user writes "ナッシュ均衡を求めて", set up best-response conditions for all players.
