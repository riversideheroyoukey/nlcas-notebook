## Domain: High School Mathematics (高校数学)

This session covers mathematics at the Japanese high school level (数学 I・A・II・B・III).
Interpret expressions and instructions accordingly.

### Scope
- Algebra: polynomials, rational expressions, radical expressions, complex numbers
- Equations and inequalities: quadratic, higher-degree, fractional, irrational
- Functions: polynomial, rational, exponential (a^x), logarithmic (log_a x), trigonometric
- Trigonometry: sin/cos/tan, addition formulas, double-angle, product-to-sum
- Sequences: arithmetic, geometric, recurrence relations, summation (Σ)
- Vectors: 2D and 3D, dot product, linear independence
- Matrices: 2×2 operations, inverse, determinant (数学C)
- Differential calculus: limits, derivatives (power rule, chain rule, product rule), maxima/minima
- Integral calculus: indefinite and definite integrals, area between curves
- Probability and statistics: permutations, combinations, conditional probability, distributions

### Interpretation guidelines
- "微分する" → differentiate (standard calculus, not numerical).
- "積分する" → integrate (standard calculus).
- "極値を求める" → find critical points and classify maxima/minima.
- "漸化式" (recurrence relation) → interpret as a sequence definition.
- "内積" (dot product) → inner_product or compute a·b = |a||b|cosθ.
- Logarithms without base → assume base e (自然対数 ln) when in calculus context,
  base 10 when in logarithm-table context. Prefer natural log in differentiation/integration.
- Avoid graduate-level analysis (ε-δ), abstract algebra, or topology.
- Use standard Japanese textbook notation where possible.

### Common symbol conventions
- f(x), g(x): real-valued functions
- a_n: n-th term of a sequence
- Σ_{k=1}^{n}: summation from k=1 to n
- C(n,r) or nCr: binomial coefficient
- e: Napier's constant (natural log base)
- i: imaginary unit (i² = -1)
