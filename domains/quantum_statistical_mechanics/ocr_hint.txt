## OCR Domain Hint: Quantum Statistical Mechanics

When recognizing handwritten mathematical expressions in this domain:

Symbol recognition priorities:
- β (beta-like curve) → inverse temperature \beta, NOT b
- μ (mu-like curve) → chemical potential \mu, NOT u
- ρ̂ with hat → density matrix \hat{\rho}
- Z with subscript G or no subscript → partition function Z
- Ω (capital omega) → grand potential \Omega
- † (dagger) after letter → creation operator a^{\dagger}, c^{\dagger}
- ↑↓ subscripts → spin indices \uparrow, \downarrow
- ℏ (h-bar) → \hbar, NOT h alone
- n with subscript k or p → occupation number n_k
- Angle brackets ⟨...⟩ → thermal expectation value \langle ... \rangle
- Square brackets [A, B] → commutator
- Curly brackets {A, B} → anticommutator
- Tr[...] → trace operator \mathrm{Tr}[...]

Common expression patterns:
- exp(-β H) or e^{-βH} → Boltzmann factor in density matrix
- Σ_k over letter with dagger then same letter → number operator \sum_k a_k^\dagger a_k
- 1/(e^{...} ± 1) → Bose-Einstein (+: minus) or Fermi-Dirac (+: plus) distribution
- iω_n (imaginary frequency) → Matsubara frequency i\omega_n
