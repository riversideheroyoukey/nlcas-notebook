## Domain: Quantum Chemistry

Common symbols and their roles:
- Ψ, ψ: molecular or atomic wavefunction — function of electron coordinates r
- H, Ĥ: electronic Hamiltonian (Born-Oppenheimer approximation)
- E: total energy or orbital energy (eigenvalue of H)
- r, **r**: electron position coordinates
- R: nuclear coordinates (parameters in Born-Oppenheimer)
- α, β: spin functions (spin-up / spin-down)
- φ: molecular orbital
- ρ(r): electron density — function of r
- ℏ: reduced Planck constant (treat as scalar constant)

Typical dependency patterns:
- Wavefunctions Ψ, ψ depend on electron coordinates r (and spin)
- Electron density ρ depends on r
- Energy E is a scalar eigenvalue — does not depend on r

When the user writes "r で微分", interpret as ∂/∂r (gradient in 3D if **r** is a vector).
