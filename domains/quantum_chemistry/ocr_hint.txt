## OCR Domain Hint: Quantum Chemistry

When recognizing handwritten mathematical expressions in this domain:

Symbol recognition priorities:
- Ψ (capital psi) → wavefunction \Psi
- ψ (lowercase psi) → orbital \psi
- φ (phi) → molecular orbital \phi or \varphi
- ∇² → Laplacian \nabla^2 (kinetic energy operator)
- ℏ (h-bar) → \hbar
- Bra-ket notation: ⟨φ|H|ψ⟩ → \langle \phi | H | \psi \rangle
- ρ(r) → electron density \rho(\mathbf{r})
- α, β (small, near spin context) → spin functions \alpha, \beta

Common expression patterns:
- Hat over letter (Ĥ) → operator \hat{H}
- Integral with dr³ or dV → three-dimensional spatial integral
- Subscript 0 on energy → ground state E_0
