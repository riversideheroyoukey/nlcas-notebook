## Domain: Signal Processing

Common symbols and their roles:
- x(t), y(t): continuous-time signals — functions of t
- x[n], y[n]: discrete-time signals — functions of integer n
- X(ω), X(f): Fourier transform of x (frequency domain)
- X(z): Z-transform of x[n]
- X(s): Laplace transform of x(t)
- h(t), h[n]: impulse response — function of t or n
- H(ω), H(s): transfer function (frequency/s-domain)
- ω: angular frequency (rad/s)
- f: frequency (Hz)
- * : convolution operator (NOT multiplication)
- δ(t), δ[n]: Dirac delta / Kronecker delta

Typical dependency patterns:
- Time-domain signals depend on t (continuous) or n (discrete)
- Frequency-domain representations depend on ω or f (not t)
- Convolution: y(t) = (x * h)(t) = ∫ x(τ)h(t-τ) dτ

When the user writes "フーリエ変換して", interpret as X(ω) = ∫ x(t) e^{-iωt} dt.
