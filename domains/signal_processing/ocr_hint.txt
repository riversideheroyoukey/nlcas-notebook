## OCR Domain Hint: Signal Processing

Symbol recognition priorities:
- X(ω) or X(jω) → Fourier transform \hat{X}(\omega) or X(j\omega)
- X(s) → Laplace transform X(s)
- X(z) → Z-transform X(z)
- * between two functions → convolution \ast, NOT multiplication
- δ(t) → Dirac delta \delta(t)
- δ[n] → Kronecker delta \delta[n]
- x[n] (with square brackets) → discrete-time signal x[n]
- ω₀, f₀ → fundamental frequency \omega_0, f_0
- H(e^{jω}) → discrete-time frequency response

Common expression patterns:
- X(ω) = ∫_{-∞}^{∞} x(t) e^{-jωt} dt → Fourier transform definition
- y(t) = x(t) * h(t) → convolution
