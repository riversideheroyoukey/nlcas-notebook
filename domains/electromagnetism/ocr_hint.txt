## OCR Domain Hint: Electromagnetism

Symbol recognition priorities:
- Bold E, B, H, D, J, A → vector fields \mathbf{E}, \mathbf{B}, etc.
- ∇× (nabla cross) → curl \nabla \times
- ∇· (nabla dot) → divergence \nabla \cdot
- ε₀ → \varepsilon_0 (NOT epsilon alone)
- μ₀ → \mu_0
- ρ in this context → charge density \rho, NOT fluid density
- φ (phi, scalar) → electric potential \varphi or \phi
- ∮ → surface/line integral \oint

Common expression patterns:
- ∇ × B = μ₀J + μ₀ε₀ ∂E/∂t → Maxwell–Ampère law
- ∇ · E = ρ/ε₀ → Gauss's law
- Double line integral with dS → surface integral \iint ... d\mathbf{S}
