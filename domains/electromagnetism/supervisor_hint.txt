## Domain: Electromagnetism

Common symbols and their roles:
- E, **E**: electric field vector — function of (x,y,z,t)
- B, **B**: magnetic flux density — function of (x,y,z,t)
- D, **D**: electric displacement field
- H, **H**: magnetic field intensity
- ρ: charge density (scalar) — NOT fluid density
- J, **J**: current density vector
- φ, V: scalar electric potential
- A, **A**: magnetic vector potential
- ε₀: permittivity of free space (constant)
- μ₀: permeability of free space (constant)
- c = 1/√(ε₀μ₀): speed of light (constant)

Typical dependency patterns:
- Field quantities E, B, D, H, J depend on (x, y, z, t)
- Potentials φ, A depend on (x, y, z, t)
- Charge/current sources ρ, J depend on (x, y, z, t)

Operator conventions:
- ∇ · E: divergence of E
- ∇ × B: curl of B
- ∇φ: gradient of scalar potential
- ∇²: Laplacian operator
