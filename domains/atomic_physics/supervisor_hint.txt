## Domain: Atomic Physics & Spectroscopy

Electronic structure of atoms and the spectra it produces: term symbols, fine and
hyperfine structure, the Zeeman effect, and radiative transitions.  Uses the Maxima
backend; the angular-momentum algebra itself (F-9) is computed exactly outside the
CAS and its results come back as ordinary expressions that Maxima can then use.

Common symbols and their roles:
- L, S, J: orbital, spin and total electronic angular momentum (quantum numbers).
- I: nuclear spin.  F = I + J: total atomic angular momentum (hyperfine).
- m_J, m_F, M: magnetic (projection) quantum numbers.
- n, l: principal and orbital quantum numbers of a one-electron state.
- ^{2S+1}L_J: term symbol, e.g. ^2P_{3/2}, ^3P_0, ^1S_0.  L is a LETTER: S,P,D,F,G,…
- g_J, g_F: Landé g factors.  g_L ≈ 1, g_S ≈ 2 (2.0023 with the QED correction).
- μ_B: Bohr magneton.  μ_N: nuclear magneton.  a_0: Bohr radius.  α: fine-structure constant.
- ζ(n,l) or ζ_{nl}: spin-orbit coupling constant.  A, B: magnetic-dipole and
  electric-quadrupole hyperfine constants.
- R_∞: Rydberg constant.  E_n: level energy.  ω_0, λ: transition frequency / wavelength.
- T^{(k)}_q: spherical tensor operator of rank k.  k=1 is the electric dipole.
- ⟨j'‖T^{(k)}‖j⟩: reduced matrix element (Wigner–Eckart).

Typical structure:
- Spin-orbit (fine structure): H_so = ζ L·S,  ⟨L·S⟩ = ½[J(J+1) − L(L+1) − S(S+1)].
- Hyperfine (magnetic dipole): H_hf = A I·J,  ⟨I·J⟩ = ½[F(F+1) − I(I+1) − J(J+1)].
  Both are the SAME identity — the Landé interval rule follows from it.
- Zeeman (weak field): H_Z = g_J μ_B B m_J.  Strong field (Paschen–Back): L and S decouple.
- Selection rules for an electric-dipole (k=1) transition: Δl = ±1, ΔJ = 0, ±1
  (not 0→0), ΔM = 0, ±1 — all of them consequences of the Wigner–Eckart
  coefficient vanishing, not separate rules to memorize.

Operation mapping (natural language → operation):
- 角運動量の合成 / 「L=1 と S=1/2 を合成して」 → couple: registers the allowed
  total J AND the exact L·S identity (with J symbolic, ready to substitute).
- クレブシュ・ゴルダン係数 / CG係数 → "j1=…, m1=…, j2=…, m2=…, J=…, M=… のCG係数"
- 3j/6j/9j記号 → "6j記号 {1 2 3; 2 1 2}" (rows separated by ';')
- 結合状態を非結合基底で展開 → "j1=…, j2=…, J=…, M=… を非結合基底で展開して"
- ランデのg因子 → "^2P_{3/2} のランデのg因子" or "L=1, S=1/2, J=3/2 のg因子"
- Wigner-Eckart / 遷移行列要素・選択則 → "j=…, m=…, k=1, q=…, j'=…, m'=… の
  Wigner-Eckartの定理"; a vanishing coefficient IS the selection rule.
- 微細構造・超微細構造の準位エネルギー → couple で L·S / I·J の恒等式を出し、
  両辺に結合定数 ζ / A を掛け、J（または F）に値を代入する（一般操作の組み合わせ）。

Notes:
- The angular-momentum layer supplies EXACT values (√ kept symbolic: 1/√2, √6/3),
  never decimals, and never invents a Hamiltonian: it gives ⟨L·S⟩ as a function of
  the total J and leaves the coupling constant and the model to the researcher.
- Half-integers are written "3/2" in instructions, not 1.5.
- Term symbols: multiplicity 2S+1 as the superscript, J as the subscript —
  ^2P_{3/2} means S=1/2, L=1, J=3/2.
