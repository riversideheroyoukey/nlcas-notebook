## Domain: Econometrics

Common symbols and their roles:
- y: dependent variable (n×1 vector of observations)
- X: design matrix / regressor matrix (n×k)
- β: coefficient vector (k×1) — parameters to estimate
- ε: error term vector (n×1)
- β̂: OLS estimator = (X'X)^{-1}X'y
- Σ, Ω: covariance matrix of errors
- R²: coefficient of determination
- σ²: error variance
- t: t-statistic; F: F-statistic
- n: sample size; k: number of regressors

Model: y = Xβ + ε

Typical dependency patterns:
- y depends on regressors in X
- Residuals ε depend on the model specification

Estimation assumptions:
- E[ε|X] = 0: zero conditional mean
- Var[ε|X] = σ²I: homoscedasticity

When the user writes "OLS推定量を求めて", interpret as β̂ = (X'X)^{-1}X'y.
