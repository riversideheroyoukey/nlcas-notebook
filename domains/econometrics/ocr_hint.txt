## OCR Domain Hint: Econometrics

Symbol recognition priorities:
- β with hat (β̂) → OLS estimator \hat{\beta}
- ε or e (small, in regression context) → error term \varepsilon
- X (uppercase, in regression) → design matrix \mathbf{X}
- X' or X^T → transpose of design matrix
- (X'X)^{-1} → inverse of moment matrix
- Σ (uppercase sigma, as matrix) → covariance matrix \Sigma
- R² → coefficient of determination R^2
- σ² → error variance \sigma^2
- t_{n-k} → t-distribution with n-k degrees of freedom

Common expression patterns:
- y = Xβ + ε → linear regression model
- β̂ = (X'X)^{-1}X'y → OLS estimator formula
- Var(β̂) = σ²(X'X)^{-1} → variance of OLS estimator
