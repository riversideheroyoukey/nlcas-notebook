## OCR Domain Hint: Quantum Field Theory

Symbol recognition priorities:
- φ (phi, cursive) → scalar field \phi or \varphi
- ψ (psi) → spinor field \psi
- π with no subscript in field theory context → conjugate momentum \pi
- ∂μ, ∂_μ → partial derivative \partial_\mu
- γμ → Dirac gamma matrix \gamma^\mu
- ℒ (script L) → Lagrangian density \mathcal{L}
- δ(x-y) or δ³(x-y) → Dirac delta \delta^{(3)}(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y})
- :...: (colon-delimited) → normal ordering \normord{\cdots}
- T(...) in context → time ordering T(\cdots)
- Square brackets [A, B] → commutator (equal-time)
- Curly brackets {A, B} → anticommutator (fermions)
- ⟨0|...|0⟩ → vacuum expectation value \langle 0 | \cdots | 0 \rangle
