Consider the Larmor precession of a spin-1/2 in a magnetic field. Prepare the spin operator $S$ (components $S_x,S_y,S_z$).

S as the spin operator

\[
  S, S^{x}, S^{y}, S^{z}
\]

Prepare the gyromagnetic ratio $\gamma$ and the magnetic field strength $B$ as real scalar parameters.

Define gamma, B as real scalars

\[
  \gamma, B
\]

Register the Zeeman Hamiltonian $H=-\gamma BS_z$ (the energy of a spin in a magnetic field).

$H = -\gamma B Sz_1$

\begin{equation}
  H = -\gamma B Sz_1
  \label{eq:E1}
\end{equation}

Compute the commutator of the Hamiltonian $H$ with the $x$-component of spin $S_x$. This commutator determines part of the time evolution of $S_x$ (QuantumAlgebra uses the Pauli-matrix convention $[S_x,S_y]=2iS_z$, so the coefficient is twice the textbook $iS_z$).

Compute the commutator of H and $Sx_1$

\begin{equation}
  - 2 B \gamma \mathit{i} Sy_1
  \label{eq:E2}
\end{equation}

Likewise, compute the commutator of the Hamiltonian $H$ with the $y$-component of spin $S_y$.

Compute the commutator of H and $Sy_1$

\begin{equation}
  2 B \gamma \mathit{i} Sx_1
  \label{eq:E3}
\end{equation}

Use the Heisenberg equation $dS_x/dt=i[H,S_x]$ to find the equation of motion for $S_x$. Precession should produce a term proportional to $S_y$.

Heisenberg equation of H for $Sx_1$

\begin{equation}
  \frac{d Sx_1}{d t} = 2 B \gamma Sy_1
  \label{eq:E4}
\end{equation}

Likewise, find the equation of motion for $S_y$. A term proportional to $S_x$, with the opposite sign, should appear.

Heisenberg equation of H for $Sy_1$

\begin{equation}
  \frac{d Sy_1}{d t} = - 2 B \gamma Sx_1
  \label{eq:E5}
\end{equation}

Find the equation of motion for $S_z$. Since $H$ is written purely in terms of $S_z$, $S_z$ should be conserved and this should vanish.

Heisenberg equation of H for $Sz_1$

\begin{equation}
  \frac{d Sz_1}{d t} = 0
  \label{eq:E6}
\end{equation}

Directly compute the commutator of $H$ and $S_z$ to confirm, by another route, the conservation result from the previous step.

Compute the commutator of H and $Sz_1$

\begin{equation}
  0
  \label{eq:E7}
\end{equation}
