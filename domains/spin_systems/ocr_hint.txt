## OCR Domain Hint: Spin Systems

Symbol recognition priorities:
- Sx, Sy, Sz → spin operators S_x, S_y, S_z
- S with + or - superscript → raising/lowering \hat{S}^+, \hat{S}^-
- σ with subscript x/y/z → Pauli matrices \sigma_x, \sigma_y, \sigma_z
- J (italic, coupling context) → exchange constant J
- ⟨i,j⟩ subscript on Σ → nearest-neighbour sum \sum_{\langle i,j \rangle}
- ↑↓ alone or as subscripts → spin-up/down \uparrow, \downarrow
- Δ (Delta) in XXZ context → anisotropy parameter \Delta
- Γ (Gamma) in transverse field context → transverse field \Gamma
- ε_{ijk} → Levi-Civita symbol \varepsilon_{ijk}
- Square brackets [Si, Sj] → commutator of spin operators
- |↑⟩, |↓⟩ → basis states \ket{\uparrow}, \ket{\downarrow}
