## OCR Domain Hint: Probability & Statistics

Symbol recognition priorities:
- E[...] or E(...) → expectation \mathbb{E}[...]
- P(...) → probability \mathbb{P}(...)
- P(A|B) (vertical bar inside) → conditional probability P(A \mid B)
- Var[X] or Var(X) → variance \text{Var}[X]
- σ² → variance; σ → standard deviation
- N(μ, σ²) → normal distribution \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)
- ρ in statistics context → correlation \rho, NOT density
- Φ (capital phi) → standard normal CDF \Phi
- ~ (tilde) between variable and distribution → "distributed as" \sim

Common expression patterns:
- E[X] = ∫ x f(x) dx → expectation integral
- P(A ∩ B) = P(A|B)P(B) → chain rule of probability
