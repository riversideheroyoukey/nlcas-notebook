## Domain: Probability & Statistics

Common symbols and their roles:
- P(A): probability of event A
- P(A|B): conditional probability of A given B
- E[X]: expectation (expected value) of random variable X
- Var[X] = E[X²] - (E[X])²: variance
- σ = √Var[X]: standard deviation
- Cov(X,Y): covariance
- ρ(X,Y): correlation coefficient
- f(x), p(x): probability density / mass function
- F(x): cumulative distribution function
- N(μ, σ²): normal distribution with mean μ, variance σ²
- Φ: standard normal CDF

Common distributions:
- X ~ N(μ, σ²): normal
- X ~ Exp(λ): exponential
- X ~ Bin(n, p): binomial
- X ~ Pois(λ): Poisson

Typical dependency patterns:
- Density functions f(x) depend on the realization x
- Parameters μ, σ, λ are scalar constants

When the user writes "期待値を計算して", interpret as E[X] = ∫ x f(x) dx.
