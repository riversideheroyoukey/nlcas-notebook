## OCR Domain Hint: Relativity

Symbol recognition priorities:
- Greek letter pairs with subscript+superscript (gμν, Γ^ρ_μν) → tensor components
- η with two subscripts → Minkowski metric \eta_{\mu\nu}
- Γ (capital gamma, with indices) → Christoffel symbol \Gamma^{\rho}_{\mu\nu}
- R with 4 indices → Riemann tensor R^{\rho}{}_{\sigma\mu\nu}
- T with two subscripts → stress-energy tensor T_{\mu\nu}
- Repeated index (one up, one down) → Einstein summation (no explicit Σ needed)
- □ or ∂²/∂t² - ∇² → d'Alembertian operator \Box

Common expression patterns:
- G_μν = 8πG T_μν → Einstein field equations
- ds² = g_μν dx^μ dx^ν → line element
