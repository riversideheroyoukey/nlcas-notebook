## OCR Domain Hint: Nuclear Physics

Symbol recognition priorities:
- † (dagger) after letter → creation operator a^{\dagger}, c^{\dagger}
- τ with subscript z → isospin \tau_z
- j with subscript → angular momentum quantum number j
- ε (epsilon, subscript k or j) → single-particle energy \varepsilon_k
- Δ (Delta, uppercase) in pairing context → gap parameter \Delta
- α with † → quasiparticle creation \alpha^{\dagger}
- ↑↓ subscripts → spin-up/spin-down \uparrow, \downarrow
- G (italic, uppercase) in interaction context → pairing strength G
- ℏ (h-bar) → \hbar
- Curly brackets {A, B} → anticommutator
- Square brackets [A, B] → commutator
- ⟨...⟩ with 0 or HF subscript → vacuum / Hartree-Fock expectation value
