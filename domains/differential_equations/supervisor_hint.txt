## Domain: Differential Equations

Common symbols and their roles:
- y(x), u(x,t): unknown function (solution)
- y', y'', y^(n): ordinary derivatives
- ∂u/∂t, ∂²u/∂x²: partial derivatives
- L, D: differential operator (e.g., L = d²/dx² + p(x)d/dx + q(x))
- G(x, ξ): Green's function
- λ: eigenvalue of a Sturm-Liouville problem
- Boundary conditions: u(0)=0, u(L)=0 etc.
- W[y₁, y₂]: Wronskian determinant

ODE types:
- Linear: L[y] = f(x)
- Homogeneous: L[y] = 0
- Initial value: y(x₀) = y₀, y'(x₀) = y₁

PDE types:
- Heat equation: ∂u/∂t = κ ∂²u/∂x²
- Wave equation: ∂²u/∂t² = c² ∇²u
- Laplace: ∇²u = 0; Poisson: ∇²u = f

Typical dependency patterns:
- ODE solutions y depend on single variable x or t
- PDE solutions u depend on spatial coordinates and t
