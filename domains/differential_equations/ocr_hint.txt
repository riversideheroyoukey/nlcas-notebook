## OCR Domain Hint: Differential Equations

Symbol recognition priorities:
- y', y'', f' → first/second derivative (prime notation) y', y''
- ẏ, ÿ (overdot) → time derivative \dot{y}, \ddot{y}
- ∂/∂t, ∂/∂x → partial derivatives \partial/\partial t
- ∇² or Δ → Laplacian \nabla^2
- G(x, ξ) with two arguments → Green's function G(x, \xi)
- W[y₁, y₂] → Wronskian \mathcal{W}[y_1, y_2]
- L[y] (operator acting on function) → differential operator L[y]
- Boundary condition notation: y(0)=0, y'(L)=0

Common expression patterns:
- ay'' + by' + cy = f(x) → second-order linear ODE
- ∂u/∂t = κ ∂²u/∂x² → heat equation
- ∂²u/∂t² = c²∇²u → wave equation
