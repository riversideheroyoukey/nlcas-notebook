## OCR Domain Hint: Quantum Mechanics

Symbol recognition priorities:
- ℏ (h-bar) → \hbar, NOT h
- Ψ (capital psi) → \Psi (wavefunction)
- ψ (lowercase psi) → \psi
- Hat over letter (Â) → operator \hat{A}
- ⟨ψ| and |φ⟩ → bra \langle \psi | and ket | \varphi \rangle
- ⟨ψ|A|φ⟩ → matrix element \langle \psi | \hat{A} | \varphi \rangle
- [A, B] → commutator \left[ \hat{A}, \hat{B} \right]
- σ with subscript x/y/z → Pauli matrices \sigma_x, \sigma_y, \sigma_z
- ∇² → \nabla^2 (kinetic energy operator term)

Common expression patterns:
- iℏ ∂ψ/∂t = Ĥψ → time-dependent Schrödinger equation
- Ĥψ = Eψ → time-independent Schrödinger equation
