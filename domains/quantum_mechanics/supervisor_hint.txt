## Domain: Quantum Mechanics

Common symbols and their roles:
- Ψ, ψ: wavefunction — depends on (x,t) or (r,t) or discrete indices
- ℏ: reduced Planck constant (scalar constant)
- Ĥ: Hamiltonian operator
- p̂ = -iℏ∇: momentum operator
- x̂: position operator
- E: energy eigenvalue (scalar)
- |ψ⟩, ⟨ψ|: Dirac ket/bra notation
- ⟨A⟩: expectation value of operator A
- [A, B] = AB - BA: commutator
- σ: Pauli matrices (σₓ, σᵧ, σ_z)

Typical dependency patterns:
- Wavefunction Ψ depends on (x,t) or (r,t)
- Stationary states ψ_n depend on x or r (not t)
- Operators act on Hilbert space — do not have classical dependencies

When the user writes "H に対する固有値方程式", interpret as Ĥψ = Eψ (time-independent Schrödinger).

Angular-momentum addition (F-9) is available here and is computed exactly, outside
the CAS:
- 角運動量の合成 →「j1=1 と j2=1/2 を合成して」: allowed total J, plus the exact
  identity ⟨j1·j2⟩ = ½[J(J+1) − j1(j1+1) − j2(j2+1)] with J left symbolic.
- クレブシュ・ゴルダン係数 →「j1=…, m1=…, j2=…, m2=…, J=…, M=… のCG係数」
- 結合状態の展開 →「j1=1/2, j2=1/2, J=0, M=0 を非結合基底で展開して」
  (singlet/triplet: the exact ±1/√2 combinations)
- 3j/6j/9j 記号、Wigner-Eckart の定理（選択則・遷移行列要素）
Half-integer quantum numbers are written "3/2" in instructions, never 1.5.
