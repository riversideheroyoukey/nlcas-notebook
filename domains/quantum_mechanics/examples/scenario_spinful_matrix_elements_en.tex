Expand a field operator in a \textbf{spin-dependent basis}: $\hat\psi_\sigma(\mathbf x)=\sum_m \varphi_{m\sigma}(\mathbf x)\hat c_{m\sigma}$. The coefficients are then all-space integrals BY DEFINITION. The basis is arbitrary, so there is nothing to carry out — the integral IS the definition. What the notebook has to offer is not "it computes" but "it can be written down and used afterwards".

Orthonormality first. $\int d^3x$ is a volume measure, not a one-variable integral.

$\int d^3x \varphi^{\ast}_{m,s}(x) \varphi_{n,t}(x)$

\begin{equation}
  \int d^3x \varphi^{\ast}_{m,s}(x) \varphi_{n,t}(x)
  \label{eq:E1}
\end{equation}

Open it into components. A threefold iterated integral, over all space even though no range was written. Spin ($s$, $t$) rides along separated from the mode — without the separator it becomes "one index called ms", and the display can no longer be typed back in.

expand into components

\begin{equation}
  \int_{-\infty }^{\infty }{\int_{-\infty }^{\infty }{\int_{-\infty }^{\infty }{\varphi_{n,t}(x_{x},x_{y},x_{z}) \varphi^{\ast}_{m,s}(x_{x},x_{y},x_{z})\;dx_{z}}\;dx_{y}}\;dx_{x}}
  \label{eq:E2}
\end{equation}

The one-body matrix element. $T$ is the one-body operator (kinetic plus external potential). The $\varphi_{m,s}$ in the integrand has to open into three arguments too — unopened, Maxima reads it as a constant in each component and returns $x_x x_y x_z \cdot \varphi$.

$h_{m,s,n,t} = \int d^3x \varphi^{\ast}_{m,s}(x) T(x) \varphi_{n,t}(x)$

\begin{equation}
  h_{m,s,n,t} = \int d^3x \varphi^{\ast}_{m,s}(x) T(x) \varphi_{n,t}(x)
  \label{eq:E3}
\end{equation}

expand into components

\begin{equation}
  \int_{-\infty }^{\infty }{\int_{-\infty }^{\infty }{\int_{-\infty }^{\infty }{T\left(x_{x} , x_{y} , x_{z}\right) \varphi_{n,t}(x_{x},x_{y},x_{z}) \varphi^{\ast}_{m,s}(x_{x},x_{y},x_{z})\;dx_{z}}\;dx_{y}}\;dx_{x}}
  \label{eq:E4}
\end{equation}

Now ask for it to be evaluated. \textbf{Unchanged is the right answer} — the basis is arbitrary, so this integral is a definition and stays one. An expansion that also evaluated would fall over here (measured: Maxima returns `ok=False` with an empty answer for an arbitrary-basis matrix element over all space).

evaluate E4

\begin{equation}
  \int_{-\infty }^{\infty }{\int_{-\infty }^{\infty }{\int_{-\infty }^{\infty }{T\left(x_{x} , x_{y} , x_{z}\right) \varphi_{n,t}(x_{x},x_{y},x_{z}) \varphi^{\ast}_{m,s}(x_{x},x_{y},x_{z})\;dx_{z}}\;dx_{y}}\;dx_{x}}
  \label{eq:E5}
\end{equation}

The two-body matrix element: \textbf{six-dimensional} ($d^3x\,d^3y$), with four spins ($s,t,u,w$). Watch $x$ and $y$ open into separate triples without mixing.

$V_{m,s,n,t,p,u,q,w} = \int d^3x \int d^3y \varphi^{\ast}_{m,s}(x) \varphi^{\ast}_{n,t}(y) v(x,y) \varphi_{p,u}(y) \varphi_{q,w}(x)$

\begin{equation}
  V_{m,s,n,t,p,u,q,w} = \int d^3x \int d^3y \varphi^{\ast}_{m,s}(x) \varphi^{\ast}_{n,t}(y) v(x,y) \varphi_{p,u}(y) \varphi_{q,w}(x)
  \label{eq:E6}
\end{equation}

expand into components

\begin{equation}
  \int_{-\infty }^{\infty }{\int_{-\infty }^{\infty }{\int_{-\infty }^{\infty }{\varphi_{q,w}(x_{x},x_{y},x_{z}) \varphi^{\ast}_{m,s}(x_{x},x_{y},x_{z}) \int_{-\infty }^{\infty }{\int_{-\infty }^{\infty }{\int_{-\infty }^{\infty }{v\left(x_{x} , x_{y} , x_{z} , y_{x} , y_{y} , y_{z}\right) \varphi_{p,u}(y_{x},y_{y},y_{z}) \varphi^{\ast}_{n,t}(y_{x},y_{y},y_{z})\;dy_{z}}\;dy_{y}}\;dy_{x}}\;dx_{z}}\;dx_{y}}\;dx_{x}}
  \label{eq:E7}
\end{equation}
