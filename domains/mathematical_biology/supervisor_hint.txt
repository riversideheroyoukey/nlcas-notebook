## Domain: Mathematical Biology

Common symbols and their roles:
- N(t), x(t): population size — function of t
- r: intrinsic growth rate (constant)
- K: carrying capacity (constant)
- α, β, γ: interaction / rate coefficients
- P(t), H(t): predator and prey populations
- u(x, t): spatial population density — depends on (x, t)
- D: diffusion coefficient (constant)
- f(N): per-capita growth rate

Common models:
- Logistic: dN/dt = rN(1 - N/K)
- Lotka-Volterra: dN/dt = rN - αNP, dP/dt = βNP - γP
- Reaction-diffusion: ∂u/∂t = D∇²u + f(u)

Typical dependency patterns:
- Population variables N, P, H depend on t
- Spatial density u depends on (x, t) or (x, y, t)
- Growth rates r, K, α are scalar parameters

When the user writes "平衡点を求めて", set dN/dt = 0 and solve for steady states.
