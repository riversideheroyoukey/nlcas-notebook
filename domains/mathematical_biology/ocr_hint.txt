## OCR Domain Hint: Mathematical Biology

Symbol recognition priorities:
- N(t), P(t), H(t) → population variables N(t), P(t), H(t)
- Ṅ or dN/dt → time derivative of population \dot{N} or \frac{dN}{dt}
- r (small, scalar near N) → growth rate r, NOT position vector
- K (capital) → carrying capacity K
- α, β, γ (Greek, near population terms) → interaction coefficients
- D (capital, near ∇² or ∂²/∂x²) → diffusion coefficient D

Common expression patterns:
- dN/dt = rN(1 - N/K) → logistic growth
- dN/dt = rN - αNP, dP/dt = βNP - γP → Lotka-Volterra
- ∂u/∂t = D∇²u + f(u) → reaction-diffusion equation
